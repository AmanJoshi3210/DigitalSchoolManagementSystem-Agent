# DSMS Student Portal Guide

This guide describes exactly how the Digital School Management System (DSMS) student portal works.
Page links are portal routes. Write them as markdown links, e.g. [My Profile](/student/profile).

## Navigation
The left sidebar (the ☰ menu button on mobile) has: Dashboard (/student/dashboard), My Profile (/student/profile),
Documents (/student/documents), Programs (/student/programs), Messages (/student/messages).
The bell icon in the top bar shows notifications (application/document decisions, new messages).

## Registering and signing in
- Register at /register. Required: Username, Email, Password (at least 8 characters), First Name, Last Name,
  Admission Number, Roll Number, Grade, Section, Admission Date. Optional: phone, date of birth, gender, address,
  blood group, guardian name and phone.
- Sign in at /login with your username or email. Make sure the **Student** portal is selected (not Staff).
- There is no "forgot password" feature yet - contact school staff if you are locked out.

## Profile and education level
- Open [My Profile](/student/profile) and click **Edit Profile** to change first/last name, phone, address,
  date of birth, gender and **Education Level** (Undergraduate, Graduate or Post Graduate). Click save when done.
- Academic Information (admission number, roll number, grade, section, admission date, guardian, blood group)
  is managed by staff. To change it, message staff from [Messages](/student/messages).
- **You must set your Education Level before you can apply to any program.** It decides which programs you
  are eligible for.

## Uploading a profile photo
- Go to [My Profile](/student/profile) and click the **camera button on your profile picture (avatar)**
  ("Change profile photo"), then choose an image.
- Allowed formats: **JPEG, PNG, WEBP or GIF**. Maximum size: 10 MB.
- Use a clear, recent, front-facing photo of your face (passport-style), with a plain background.
- Profile photos are accepted immediately - they do not go through staff review.
- Uploading a new photo replaces the old one.
- Do NOT upload the profile photo from the Documents page - it is only uploaded from My Profile.

## Uploading documents
- Go to [Documents](/student/documents) and click **Upload Document**.
- Choose the **Document Type**: Passport, Offer Letter, Certificate, or Other. Pick the file and optionally add a
  description (e.g. "Class 12 mark sheet"). Click **Upload**.
- Maximum file size: **10 MB** per file. PDF, JPG or PNG are recommended; scans must be clear and complete
  (all pages, all corners visible, readable text).
- If you have several certificates, upload each one separately with type **Certificate** and a description.
- After upload, a student document is **Pending** until school staff review it. Staff then mark it
  **Approved** (verified) or **Rejected** (with a reason). You get a notification either way.
- If a document is rejected, the reason is shown under the document on the Documents page. Fix the problem
  and upload a corrected file as a new document. You can delete the rejected one with the trash button.
- Click **View** on any document to open it.

## Programs and applying
- Open [Programs](/student/programs) to see all available programs, the eligible education level, the
  application deadline and whether you are eligible ("Eligible", "Not Eligible", or "Set education level").
  A "Closed" badge means the program is no longer accepting applications.
- Click a program name to open its detail page, then click **Apply Now**.
- You can apply only if ALL of these are true:
  1. Your Education Level is set in your profile.
  2. Your Education Level is exactly the program's eligible level.
  3. The program is active and today is on or before the application deadline.
  4. You have not already applied to that program (you can apply to each program only once).
- The school also expects the required documents (see "required documents" from the assistant's tools) to be
  uploaded so staff can verify your application. Upload them **before** applying - staff review documents
  and applications together.
- After applying, the status is **Pending**. Staff will **Approve** or **Reject** it; you get a notification,
  and rejections include the reason when staff give one. Track statuses under "My Applications" on the
  Programs page.
- An application cannot be withdrawn or edited from the portal - message staff if you applied by mistake.

## Messages and getting more help
- Open [Messages](/student/messages) and click **New Message**.
  - **Message Staff**: a direct conversation with a staff member.
  - **Raise a Query**: a tracked question with a subject (e.g. "Documents for the Exchange Program").
    Staff can mark it Resolved or Closed. Replying to a Resolved query reopens it.
- For anything this assistant cannot answer (fees, special cases, changing academic details, deadline
  extensions), raise a Query with staff.
